MCP 服务已下线

由于资源被滥用，薄荷输入法对外 MCP 服务长期日请求量达 120 万次，产生的持续开支已无法承担，因此决定下线该服务。

此地址仅保留下线说明，不再提供 MCP 功能。请删除或禁用客户端中指向 https://www.mintimate.cc/mcp 的 MCP 配置（通常名为 oh-my-rime-knowledge），避免继续连接和重试。

感谢每一位使用过这项服务、支持薄荷输入法的朋友。如果它曾帮你解决一个配置问题，或让你少走一点弯路，这次尝试就有了意义。

很遗憾，MCP 服务只能陪大家走到这里了。谢谢这一路的相伴，我们在薄荷输入法的文档里再见。愿你今后的每一次输入，都顺手、顺心。

MCP service retired

The public Oh My Rime MCP service has been shut down due to resource abuse, sustained traffic of 1,200,000 requests per day, and unaffordable operating costs.

This address now serves only a retirement notice. Please remove or disable the MCP entry pointing to https://www.mintimate.cc/mcp in your client (usually named oh-my-rime-knowledge) to prevent further connection attempts and retries.

Thank you to everyone who used this service and supported Oh My Rime. If it helped you solve a configuration problem or saved you a little trial and error, the effort was worthwhile.

We're sorry to say goodbye to the MCP service. Thank you for being part of its journey. We'll see you in the Oh My Rime documentation, and we hope your everyday typing stays smooth and enjoyable.
